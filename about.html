About Heresy Gaming Network

Who we are, what we stand for, and how we play

What is Heresy Gaming Network?

Heresy Gaming Network (HGN) is a performance-tuned community cluster hosted on our own custom homelab hardware. We focus on delivering stable, mod-friendly servers with transparent maintenance schedules and quick recovery when issues arise.

Our mission is to create an inclusive gaming environment where players can enjoy their favorite games without the typical hosting headaches. We believe in community-driven management, fair play, and technical excellence.

  • Self-hosted infrastructure: Docker + Nginx reverse proxy for reliability
  • Fast patch cadence: Active monitoring and quick updates
  • Community-driven: Player feedback shapes settings and events
  • Transparent operations: Open communication about maintenance and changes

Ground Rules

Play Nice, Have Fun

These rules exist to ensure everyone has a positive gaming experience. Violations will be handled fairly but firmly.

1

No Harassment or Hate Speech

Zero tolerance policy. This includes racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, or any form of discrimination. We're here to game, not to hurt people.

2

No Cheating, Duping, or Exploits

Using hacks, exploiting game bugs, or item duplication is prohibited. If you find a bug, report it privately to admins—don't abuse it.

3

Raiding Etiquette (ARK)

PvP is allowed, but don't grief. Don't completely wipe new tribes. Leave them enough to rebuild. Repeated griefing will result in bans.

4

Keep Chat Usable

No spam, advertising, or excessive link-dumping in chat. Keep discussions relevant and respectful.

5

Respect Admins & Tickets

Staff members are volunteers. Be honest in support tickets, provide evidence when reporting issues, and respect their decisions.

6

Appropriate Names & Builds

No slurs in usernames, tribe names, or structures. Avoid lag-inducing builds (excessive redstone, massive mob farms).

7

Maintenance Happens

Expect brief scheduled downtime for updates and maintenance. We announce these in Discord #announcements ahead of time.

Consequences

Breaking rules can lead to warnings, temporary mutes, kicks, or permanent bans depending on severity. Multiple infractions will result in escalating punishments.

Appeals

Appeals are welcome if you believe a punishment was unfair. Contact admins on Discord with context and evidence. Be respectful and honest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

We post all wipe and update schedules in Discord #announcements at least 48 hours in advance. For ARK, we typically follow the official update schedule plus weekly maintenance windows.

Regular Maintenance: Sundays at 3 AM EST (brief, usually under 30 minutes)

Use the Discord ticket system:

  1. Go to the #support channel in Discord
  2. Click the "Create Ticket" button
  3. Describe the issue clearly
  4. Include screenshots, logs, or video if possible

Tip: The more evidence you provide, the faster we can resolve the issue.

Check the Live Server Status section on our home page. It shows real-time data from BattleMetrics including:

  • Online/offline status
  • Current player count
  • Server IP and port
  • Last update timestamp

You can also click any server card to view detailed statistics on BattleMetrics.
